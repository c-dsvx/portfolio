About

I'm a 3rd year PhD student currently working in the FlowersAI & CogSci lab at the INRIA research institute in Bordeaux (France). My thesis project focuses on the development and evaluation of school interventions to promote curiosity-driven learning in primary to middle school children, by leveraging metacognition.

In this work, we adopt a systemic approach towards school, specifically including teachers for participatory design of our interventions.
